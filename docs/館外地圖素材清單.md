# 館外地圖素材清單（圖書館「地圖」頁籤）

> 2026-11-16，Claude 寫。規格在 `.claude/交接/主線拓展規劃稿.md` 第伍節（5.2 四層透明片、5.4 要生的圖）。
> 網頁版（一張一段、可以直接複製）：`node tools/flat_prompts.cjs --src docs/館外地圖素材清單.md --out 館外地圖Prompt.html --dir assets/map`。清單改了就重跑，不要手改網頁。
> 生好的原圖放 `assets/map/`，處理好的成品放 `web/public/map/`。做法看 `docs/素材生成.md`（拆零件、綠底、八條）。

## 〇、先看這幾條

- 風格：手繪的舊紙地圖，米黃紙、褐色墨線、只有一點點紅（章和圖釘），像老博物館裡的一張攤開的地圖。單色加一點紅，不要寫實，不要彩色。
- 不畫青蛙、不畫任何生物的臉（人物剪影章只有黑剪影，不畫臉）。不要字、不要數字、不要國旗、不要真實地名。
- 共用的結尾：

```
flat front view, no perspective, soft light from the upper left, hand-drawn 2D game UI asset, aged cream paper, brown ink linework with a tiny touch of dull red, muted warm palette, old museum map mood, no text, no letters, no numbers, no frog, no animal, no face of any living creature
```

- 零件多接一段零件結尾（原因看 `docs/素材生成.md` 的八條）：

```
Draw ONLY the listed items. Draw exactly the number of items listed, no duplicates, no extra rows. Pure flat #00FF00 background with no gradient, no vignette and no ground shadow. Items of the same kind are the same size; lay all items out in a neat grid, evenly spaced, with at least 80 pixels of empty green between items. Different states of one item keep an identical shape and size; only the color and glow change.
```

- 四片透明片整張（L1 城鎮、L3 已知世界、L2 別的館、L4 總帳，直拿 720×1600 webp）**不生成**：用下面的零件在 `web/src/ui_map.js` 疊起來，或美術統籌用零件拼好壓成一片。
- 第一批先生 M-1、M-4、M-5（有這三樣就能排版），其他可以後補。

## 一、底

### M-1 手繪地圖紙（可拼接）

```
A seamless tileable square texture of aged hand-drawn map paper: warm cream parchment with faint fibres, a few very light brown water stains, no creases, no borders, no drawing on it, no center focus, the same tone across the whole square, edges that match when tiled. 【結尾】
```

對應：L1 和 L3 的紙底，鋪滿重複貼。注意：這張不用綠底。

### M-2 帳頁行線（可拼接，淡）

```
A seamless tileable square texture of an old ledger page: pale cream paper with thin evenly spaced horizontal ruled lines in faded brown-grey ink and one faint vertical margin line, very low contrast, no text, edges that match when tiled. 【結尾】
```

對應：L4 總帳的紙底（把紙底換成帳頁）。不用綠底。

### M-3 紙邊、折角條、四角

```
A horizontally tileable strip of torn and softly worn paper edge, about 1600 pixels wide and 120 pixels high, seen from the front, with the paper body at the bottom and the rough edge on top. Then four separate square corner pieces of the same worn paper, each with a small fold or a curled corner, one for each corner of a map sheet. Exactly five items: one strip and four corners. 【結尾】
```

對應：地圖四周的邊（邊條重複接、四角固定，直的邊用轉 90 度的）。

---

## 圖釘

### M-4 圖釘（亮、輪廓、新）三種狀態

```
Three identical map pins side by side: a round-headed pin with a short needle, hand-drawn. State one is lit: bright warm red head with a soft glow. State two is an outline only: the same shape drawn as a thin brown ink outline with empty paper-coloured inside, no glow. State three is new: the same shape in bright gold with a soft glow and tiny sparkle marks around it. Exactly three items. 【結尾】
```

對應：`ui_map.js` 的圖釘（亮字、只亮輪廓、新）。三張**同形狀只換顏色和光**，狀態換的時候才不會跳。

## 地點圖示

### M-5 地點圖示 15 個（5 × 3 格）

```
Fifteen small hand-drawn map location icons in a neat grid of five columns by three rows, one icon per cell, all the same size, simple brown ink drawing with one small touch of red, no shadows. Row one: a street lamp; an umbrella; a small shop sign hanging from a bracket; a night market string of lights with a chair; a small stone bridge. Row two: a water gate (sluice) in a canal; a letterbox; a coffee cup; a bus stop sign; a small school building. Row three: an old street signboard; a small island with a chimney; a harbour with a boat; a pawnshop counter; an empty square frame. Exactly fifteen items, in this order. 【結尾】
```

對應：地圖上的地點小圖（`spot.icon`：lamp、umbrella、shop、market、bridge、gate、mailbox、cup、stop、school、signboard、island、harbour、pawn、blank）。

## 章和人物

### M-6 章 6 個

```
Six small hand-drawn rubber-stamp marks in a grid of three columns by two rows, all the same size, dull red-brown ink. Row one: a hollow circle stamp with worn edge (light wear); the same hollow circle stamp with medium wear; the same hollow circle stamp with heavy wear and gaps. Row two: a square stamp with one corner missing; a small round stamp with two blank letter-like scribble marks inside (initials without real letters); a long narrow ledger header strip stamp with an empty round hole at the left. The two stamp shapes, the circle and the square, must be easy to tell apart at a glance. Exactly six items. 【結尾】
```

對應：L3 往來圈（空心章三種磨損）、缺角方章、人名縮寫章、分冊號頁眉條。

### M-7 人物剪影章 10 個

```
Ten round badge stamps in a grid of five columns by two rows, all the same size: a thin circular frame with a solid dark silhouette of a person inside, drawn from the shoulders up with no face detail, each carrying one different object that tells who it is. Row one: a person holding an open umbrella; a person with a postman's shoulder bag; a person holding a chisel; a person holding a lantern; a person wearing a hat. Row two: a person holding an abacus; a person holding a balance scale; a person with a long coat and a scarf; a person with a broom; a person with an apron. Exactly ten items, in this order. No faces. 【結尾】
```

對應：地點抽屜裡「住的人」的剪影章。不畫臉。

## 路徑和霧

### M-8 路徑、腳印、水線

```
Three separate hand-drawn map line pieces: a horizontally tileable dotted path line of brown ink dashes, 1200 pixels wide; a horizontally tileable row of small footprints walking along a line, 1200 pixels wide; a horizontally tileable wavy double water line in pale blue-grey ink, 1200 pixels wide. Exactly three items, each as a thin horizontal strip. 【結尾】
```

對應：L1／L2 的路徑、腳印、水閘的水線。

### M-9 邊緣霧（半透明，不用綠底）

```
A soft hand-painted fog edge to place at the border of a map: a wide horizontal band of pale cream mist, fully opaque white-cream at the bottom edge fading smoothly to completely clear at the top edge, with gentle cloudy shapes, no hard lines, no objects, on a solid pure black background so that the mist can be converted into transparency later. Exactly one item, 1600 pixels wide. flat front view, no text, no frog, no face.
```

對應：L2 別的館邊緣的霧（邊緣霧化到畫面外，不寫總數）。這張**不用綠底**：用黑底生，再轉成透明。
