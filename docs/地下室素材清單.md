# 地下室改版：要生成的素材和 Prompt

> 2026-11-12，Claude 寫。翊文 11-12：「動畫什麼的要足夠精美，需要生成素材零件也給我Prompt」。
> 做法照 `docs/素材生成.md`：拆成零件、英文指令、純綠背景、光從左上、不要有字。規劃在 `docs/地下室改版規劃.md`。
> **都還沒生成。** 生好的原圖放 `assets/cellar2/`，處理好的放 `web/public/cellar2/`。一張一張傳，等 AI 回覆再傳下一張。

---

## 〇、先看這幾條

- **每一張都附一張現有的圖當風格參考**：建議附 `web/public/lobby/land.webp`（大廳導覽圖）或地下室石板框（`assets/panel-kit/stone-raw.webp`），並在指令最前面加一句 `Match the art style of the attached reference image.`
- **怪物都是「東西」，不是動物**：紙箱、白布、畫、帳本。每一條怪物的指令都寫了 `no frog, no animal, no face of any living creature`（界線：不畫 Pepe，也不要像）。
- **神祕感**：還沒登記過的怪物，畫面上只給剪影（程式把圖變黑、加霧，不用另外生）。B5 的三隻故意畫得不清楚。
- **動畫怎麼做**：不生影片。靠三種東西組起來：
  1. 怪物一張圖裡三個姿勢（平常、出招、安靜下來），程式切換加上呼吸、晃動。
  2. 特效用 6 格的連續圖（一張圖排一列），程式一格一格播。
  3. 其他（條、按鈕、卡片）用 CSS 動。
  `body.reduce-motion` 的時候只切姿勢、不播特效。
- 下面每一段的最後一行是共用的結尾，貼的時候要一起貼：

```
flat front view, no perspective, soft light from the upper left, hand-painted 2D game asset, muted warm palette with wine red and aged gold accents, slightly eerie but cozy old museum basement mood, clean readable silhouette, no text, on a solid pure green (#00FF00) chroma key background, no checkerboard, no shadows on the background
```

以下用 **【結尾】** 代表這一段。

## 一、地圖

### 1-1 地圖的底（五層各一張，可以無縫拼接）

```
A seamless tileable square texture, 1024x1024, top-down view of an old museum basement floor: {LAYER}. Even lighting, no objects, no border, no text, hand-painted 2D game texture, muted warm palette.
```

`{LAYER}` 換成：

| 層 | 換成 |
|---|---|
| B1 | `dusty wooden floorboards with faint tiny footprints in the dust` |
| B2 | `cold grey stone tiles with thin frost in the cracks` |
| B3 | `dark green damask wallpaper pattern, slightly faded` |
| B4 | `shallow still water over old paper files, soft ripples, ink bleeding` |
| B5 | `plain off-white plaster, almost blank, very faint pencil grid lines` |

（底圖不用綠背景，不用貼【結尾】。）

### 1-2 地圖上的點（一張五個）

```
A sprite sheet of 5 round map marker icons arranged in a single row with wide empty space between each item, each about 256x256: (1) a small wooden door slightly open with warm light inside, (2) a dark blurry shape under a dusty cloth with two faint glints, (3) a small old wooden chest with a brass lock, (4) a lit oil lantern on a folded blanket, (5) a tall heavy double door with an iron ring and a wax seal. Each icon sits on a round aged-parchment disc with a thin gold rim. 【結尾】
```

對應：探索、遭遇、寶箱和怪事、休息、守門。

### 1-3 怪物等級的記號、現在的位置、今天的標籤（一張）

```
A sprite sheet arranged in a single row with wide empty space between each item: (1) one small claw scratch mark in dark red, (2) two claw scratch marks, (3) three claw scratch marks, (4) a small brass pawn-shaped pin marker, (5) a small blank hanging paper tag with a red string, (6) a small blank wax seal stamp in wine red. 【結尾】
```

對應：等級 1、2、3、我們在這裡、「今天」的標籤（字由程式寫）、已登記的印章。

### 1-4 連線（可以橫向無縫接的一條）

```
A seamless horizontally tileable strip, 512x64, a dotted trail of tiny dusty footprints walking from left to right, evenly spaced. 【結尾】
```

## 三、介面的零件

### 3-1 行動按鈕的圖示（一張六個）

```
A sprite sheet of 6 square action icons arranged in a single row with wide empty space between each item, each about 256x256, each on a dark wine-red rounded square with a thin gold border: (1) two open hands pushing forward, (2) a brass magnifying glass, (3) an open palm held up gently with a small warm glow, (4) a round wooden shield, (5) a pair of running shoes kicking up dust, (6) a staircase going up with an arrow. 【結尾】

對應：推、看穿、安撫、擋、逃跑、撤退。按鈕上的字由程式寫（規劃第八節：有框有字）。

### 3-2 招式的圖示（一張八個）

A sprite sheet of 8 small round badge icons arranged in two rows of four with wide empty space between each item, each about 192x192, each on an aged-gold round badge: (1) a baseball cap, (2) a small golden crown, (3) a dark hat with curling tentacles, (4) a pointed wizard hat with a star, (5) a knitted wool beanie, (6) a pillow with a "resting" crescent moon, (7) a low squatting stool, (8) a thumbs-up hand. 【結尾】

### 3-5 登記卡（一張兩個）

```
A sprite sheet arranged in a single row with wide empty space between the two items: (1) a blank vertical museum catalogue index card, 600x840, aged cream paper with faint ruled lines, a punched hole at the top and a small empty square frame for a picture, (2) the same card seen from the back with a blank wine-red wax seal in the corner. 【結尾】
